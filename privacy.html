Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

1. Scope

Økonomi is a privately operated personal-finance application used by its owner. It is not offered as a public banking, payment, or financial-advisory service.

2. Data processed

Depending on the features enabled, Økonomi may process financial information such as local account records, transactions, categories, bills, expected income, budgets, savings goals, and read-only bank-account information obtained through Open Banking.

3. Open Banking

When Open Banking is used, bank authentication and consent are handled through Enable Banking and the selected bank. Økonomi requests account-information access only. It does not initiate payments.

Authorization credentials, BankID credentials, private keys, authorization codes, and provider access secrets are not intended to be stored as ordinary financial records in the application.

4. Purpose of processing

Financial data is processed only to provide the owner with a personal financial overview, budgeting, forecasting, reconciliation, account linking, and related personal-finance functionality.

5. Storage

The application is designed primarily as a local-first application. Financial records are stored on infrastructure controlled by the application owner. Open Banking providers and banks may separately process data necessary to provide authorization and account-information services under their own terms and privacy notices.

6. Sharing and sale of data

Økonomi does not sell personal or financial data. Data is not intentionally shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Information is exchanged with the selected Open Banking provider and bank only as necessary to establish and operate the requested read-only bank connection.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored data is retained until the application owner removes it or the relevant local storage is deleted. Bank consent or Open Banking connections may also be revoked through the applicable bank, provider, or application controls.

8. Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to keep Open Banking secrets server-side and separate from ordinary application data. No system can guarantee absolute security, and the application is intended for private use on systems controlled by its owner.

9. Contact

For privacy or data-protection questions, use the data-protection contact address registered for the Økonomi application with Enable Banking.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as Økonomi changes. The current version and its update date are published on this page.
